Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to me and I'll get back to you.

The camera doesn't open

Markit needs camera access to scan barcodes and pages. Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Apps, then Markit, and turn on Camera.

My book isn't found

Book details come from Open Library and Google Books, which don't know every edition. Choose Enter Manually on the Add Book screen and type the title and author yourself.

Some words aren't recognized

Hold the iPhone parallel to the page, in good light, and fill the frame with the text. Turn on the torch for dim rooms, and tap the page to focus.

Where are my highlights stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account or sync, so they're included in your iPhone's backups but don't appear on other devices. To keep a copy elsewhere, open a book and use the share button to export its highlights as Markdown.

How do I delete a highlight or a book?

Swipe left on a highlight or a book and tap Delete. Deleting a book also deletes its highlights.
