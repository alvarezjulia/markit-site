Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 8 NOVEMBER 2026

Markit is an app for saving highlights from the paper books you read. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect any of your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your books and highlights. Titles, authors, covers, the words you select, page numbers, notes and the photos of the pages you capture are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are not sent to the developer or to any server. They are included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), under your Apple Account, if you have backups turned on.
  • The camera. Markit uses the camera to scan barcodes and photograph pages. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in technology. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

What leaves your iPhone

When you add a book, Markit looks it up so it can fill in the title, author and cover:

  • The ISBN from a scanned barcode, or the search words you type, are sent to Open Library (run by the Internet Archive) and, if Open Library has no match, to Google Books.
  • Cover images are downloaded from Open Library or Google.

These requests contain only the ISBN or search words. They contain no name, account, or identifier from Markit. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address when answering the request; their own privacy policies apply: Internet Archive and Google.

Your highlights, notes and page photos are never sent.

Sharing and export

If you export highlights as Markdown, Markit hands the text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is the app or person you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete a highlight or a book inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

Markit does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
